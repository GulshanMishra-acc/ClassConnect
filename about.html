WHO WE ARE

About ClassConnect

ClassConnect is a student-initiated academic resource platform designed for B.Tech students of the Institute of Engineering and Technology (IET), Deen Dayal Upadhyay Gorakhpur University (DDUGU).

Our goal is to make academic resources easily accessible and well-organized in one place. Instead of searching through multiple sources, students can find useful study materials according to their semester and subjects.
